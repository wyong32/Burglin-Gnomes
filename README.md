# Burglin' Gnomes Guide

**Live site:** [https://burglin-gnomes.org](https://burglin-gnomes.org)

Player-written guide for *Burglin' Gnomes* — a physics-heavy online co-op heist game where tiny gnomes break into human homes, complete High-Gnome jobs, steal loot, craft gear, and survive cats, humans, and household chaos. This site collects routes, item notes, crafting priorities, enemy counters, and patch notes from real runs, updated as the live game changes.

---

## About the game

*Burglin' Gnomes* launched in full release on **Steam (Windows)** on **June 10, 2026**, developed by **Fobri**. Runs support **1–6 players** in solo or online co-op.

Each day rolls **five tasks**; the squad must finish **at least three** before calling extraction at the bell. Fail the task minimum or lose the whole squad, and the run resets. The gnome-world base is where you bank materials, craft at the Well and Potion Table, revive teammates, and plan the next house route.

The core loop is simple on paper and messy in practice: enter through a reliable route (vine windows beat the front door for beginners), clear small objectives first, search storage with intent, craft upgrades that reduce future risk, and leave before greed turns a profitable run into a rescue mission.

---

## What makes Burglin' Gnomes stand out

- **Task-first heists** — Loot is tempting, but the High-Gnome checklist decides whether the day succeeds.
- **Physics-heavy interaction** — Right-click grab, arm distance, climbing, and object handling matter as much as combat.
- **Co-op route planning** — One player calls tasks, another watches threats; random splitting costs time fast.
- **Base progression** — Backpack, Pickaxe, Metal Bat, Spring Shoes, potions, and Gnomium gear change how risky the next run feels.
- **Household threats** — Humans, cats, rats, cockroaches, Evil Gnomes, Roomba hazards, and outdoor animals all punish sloppy routes.
- **Full-release tuning** — Patches can shift AI, recipes, and physics; the guide tracks what to recheck after updates.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Burglin' Gnomes co-op?**  
Yes. Steam lists online co-op for up to six players, including solo play with friends.

**When did Burglin' Gnomes release?**  
The Steam release date is June 10, 2026. Guide pages are updated as the live build changes.

**Is the game on console or mobile?**  
The Steam store page currently lists Windows PC only. No official console or mobile versions are documented on this guide.

**What should new players do first?**  
Read the task list, enter through a forgiving route like a vine window, finish three small tasks, then loot only when extraction is already safe. Start with the [Beginner Guide](https://burglin-gnomes.org/beginner).

**Why do runs fail even when loot looks good?**  
The win condition is task progress, not backpack value. Three completed tasks and a known exit matter more than one expensive carry.

**What should I craft first?**  
Usually **Backpack** for fewer trips, then **Pickaxe** for material farming and **Metal Bat** for pests. Exact order depends on the house layout and task list — see [Crafting](https://burglin-gnomes.org/crafting).

**Where do materials like Clonk and Fraggles come from?**  
Break or steal matching household objects, bank them in gnome-world storage, then spend at the Well or Potion Table. Item pages list typical rooms and route notes.

**What kills runs most often?**  
Humans, cats, greedy looting after three tasks, bad exits, co-op desync, and fighting in rooms without an escape line. The [Bestiary](https://burglin-gnomes.org/bestiary) covers specific counters.

**Is this an official Fobri or Steam site?**  
No. [burglin-gnomes.org](https://burglin-gnomes.org) is an independent player guide. Game names, images, and content belong to their respective owners.
